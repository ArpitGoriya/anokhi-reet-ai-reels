## Brief

- **About:** Nidhi's cousin Ishani goes viral at every wedding (mehendi sharara, sangeet gown, shaadi lehenga, reception
  saree). The family is sure it's sponsored. Nidhi asks; guesses ₹1-2 lakh; twist 1: all four looks on rent, ₹1,200
  each, ₹4,800 in all. Twist 2: the outfits Ishani bought earlier (already seen on her Instagram) are now rented by her
  followers: spent ₹4,800, earned ₹8,000 (Udaharan). "Influencer bhi… aur investor bhi." Badi Mummy: "meri saree bhi
  daal de na". Anokhi Reet: rent a new look, or put your old looks on rent. Comment OUTFIT.
- **For:** Young women, 18-28.
- **One message:** Rent a new look for every wedding, and put the looks you've already posted on rent. Comment "OUTFIT".
- **Voice:** female (Vindemiatrix) as Nidhi, the narrator; she also reads Ishani's, Badi Mummy's and Mama's lines
  (chat screens and speech bubbles show who speaks). Female Hindi grammar.
- **Characters:** New: **Ishani** (the stylish cousin), **Nidhi** (narrator), **Badi Mummy** (aunt), **Mama** (in the
  family chat only).
- **Length:** about 55 s (the voice decides).
- **Music:** none (voice only).
- **Special wishes:** comic-book panels with Instagram-style frames (likes, hearts, comments); the app name hidden until
  the end.
