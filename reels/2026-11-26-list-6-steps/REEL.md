# Reel: Outfit rent pe list karo, 6 steps mein 📲

- **Date started:** 2026-11-26

## Brief

- **About:** A how-to: list your outfit for rent on the Anokhi Reet app in six steps: (1) Add Product, photos (up to
  six), Continue; (2) rent price, original price, security deposit; (3) gender; (4) size, occasion, colour, city, Next
  Step; (5) title and description, or a template draft; (6) check and Review: approved within 24 hours, a WhatsApp
  confirmation, and the outfit goes live. "Aaj hi apna pehla outfit list kijiye." Download the app.
- **For:** People who want to put their outfits on rent (individuals and shops).
- **One message:** Listing an outfit takes six simple steps. Download the app.
- **Voice:** female (Vindemiatrix), as Riya from Team Anokhi Reet, explaining each step.
- **Characters:** From the cast: **Riya** (`RIYA`, the presenter from "Customer kahan hain?").
- **Length:** about 55 s (the voice decides).
- **Music:** none (voice only).
- **Special wishes:** a clear tutorial: a big phone with simple drawn screens for each step, a step counter (1/6 … 6/6),
  the tapped button highlighted each time.
