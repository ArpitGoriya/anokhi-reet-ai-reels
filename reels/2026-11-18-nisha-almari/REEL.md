## Brief

- **About:** A story reel for individual outfit owners and homemakers. Nisha (Surat) has 12 lehengas worth ₹2.5 lakh
  shut in her almirah. Selling online gets an offer of ₹3,000 for a ₹25,000 lehenga (₹2 lakh+ loss). A line from
  Rich Dad Poor Dad (asset puts money in your pocket, expense takes it out) changes how she sees them. She lists
  them for rent, prices each rental, dry-cleans after every rental; each lehenga earns back part of its cost.
- **For:** Individual outfit owners, homemakers.
- **One message:** An outfit isn't old, it's just useless locked in a cupboard. Rent it out.
- **Voice:** female
- **Characters:** new: **Nisha** (homemaker, Surat, 30s) and her husband (reuse `PATI` look in a new colour);
  customers as icons.
- **Length:** about 55-60 s (191 words)
- **Music:** none
- **Special wishes:** Comment keyword "RENT" at the end; "Rent. Earn. Repeat."
