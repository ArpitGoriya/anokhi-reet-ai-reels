## Brief

- **About:** Business lesson series "Dukaan ka Funda": price signals quality. Rekhaben keeps the cheapest rent in
  the market and still has no customers; Jignaben across the street charges a fair rent and is busy. A real study
  (Plassmann et al., 2008) shows people rate the same thing higher with a higher price. Rekhaben fixes it in three steps.
- **For:** Rental shop owners (lehengas, sherwanis).
- **One message:** Sasta rent nahi, sahi rent bikta hai. Put your shop online: comment "ONLINE".
- **Voice:** female (Vindemiatrix), as the narrator.
- **Characters:** New: **Rekhaben** (40s, rental shop owner, priced everything cheap) and **Jignaben** (40s, shop
  owner across the street, confident). Customers (reuse `CUSTOMER_W`, `GUEST_W1`, `GUEST_W2`); Rekhaben's family in
  scene 4 (reuse `PATI`); study people (reuse `LAB_M`, `LAB_W` and cast extras).
- **Length:** about 60 s (the voice decides).
- **Music:** none (voice only).
- **Special wishes:** Yellow-box highlights, emojis on screen as in the script, a "STEP 1/2/3" structure.

## Lessons

- Give a character's move (walking to the other shop or restaurant) at least a second on screen after the line that
  causes it, and keep the sign that explains it visible when they arrive.
- Whisper can miss the first seconds of a voice and shift every caption: always compare STARTS with the pauses.
