# Reel: Cupboard full, pehenne ko kuch nahi (Kavya)

- **Date started:** 2026-11-20
- **Made by:** dhruvifewtone-wq
- **Status:** script written, waiting for approval

## Brief

- **About:** A relatable story for customers. Kavya's cupboard is full, but everything has been worn and photographed
  already; Mummy says her line; buying new again means a big spend that ends up in the same cupboard. She finds a new
  look on Anokhi Reet (city, occasion, budget → outfits from listed shops → booked) and wears it at the function
  without buying.
- **For:** Customers (young women, weddings and functions).
- **One message:** Naya look, bina naya kharide. Download Anokhi Reet.
- **Voice:** female (Vindemiatrix), as the narrator (teammate chose it, 2026-11-20).
- **Characters:** New: **Kavya** (25-30) and **Mummy** (her mother, short cameo).
- **Length:** about 50 s (the voice decides).
- **Music:** none (voice only).
- **Special wishes:** Yellow-box highlights on key words, emojis on screen as in the script.

## Teammate changes (override the brand defaults for this reel only)

- Key words on screen in yellow (gold) highlight boxes; on-screen emojis as in the script.
- **Lines:** the scene version (teammate chose it, 2026-11-20): scene 2 keeps "isme to already bohot photos hain…",
  scene 6 says "aur naya kharidne ke paise bhi bache!".
- **No rent amount on the app screen** (teammate chose it, 2026-11-20): the peach lehenga's card shows the shop and
  "Rent", no number. The "₹15,000" buy-new tag is Kavya's thought, labelled "Udaharan".
- Voice only: the script's sounds (creak, thud, boing, cash register, gulp, ding, taps, pop, music, jingle) are shown
  visually; no "whoosh" (brand rule).
- Hashtags: 5 (dropped #GujaratiWedding, teammate chose it).
- The app name is said in Devanagari ("अनोखी रीत") so the voice says it properly.
- Only clothes appear as products.
