## Brief

- **About:** A relatable story for customers. Kavya's cupboard is full, but everything has been worn and photographed
  already; Mummy says her line; buying new again means a big spend that ends up in the same cupboard. She finds a new
  look on Anokhi Reet (city, occasion, budget → outfits from listed shops → booked) and wears it at the function
  without buying.
- **For:** Customers (young women, weddings and functions).
- **One message:** Naya look, bina naya kharide. Download Anokhi Reet.
- **Voice:** female (Vindemiatrix), as the narrator (teammate chose it, 2026-11-20).
- **Characters:** New: **Kavya** (25-30) and **Mummy** (her mother, short cameo).
- **Length:** about 50 s (the voice decides).
- **Music:** none (voice only).
- **Special wishes:** Yellow-box highlights on key words, emojis on screen as in the script.

## Lessons

- Curiosity recipe for customer stories: promise a payoff right after the hook, keep the trick secret ("? ? ?"
  header) until about two-thirds in, then close the promise near the end.
- A growing pile must stop below the top label.
