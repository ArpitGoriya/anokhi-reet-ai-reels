# Reel: Hetal ben's lehenga (sunk cost fallacy)

- **Date started:** 2026-11-13
- **Made by:** Deep
- **Status:** preview 1 exported (2026-11-13); waiting for feedback

## Brief

- **About:** A story reel. Hetal ben keeps re-wearing an expensive lehenga "to get her money's worth" (sunk cost
  fallacy), then rents it out and rents new looks instead.
- **For:** Women who buy costly lehengas for family weddings.
- **One message:** A lehenga's value comes from how often it's worn, not its price tag. Rent it out, rent new looks.
- **Voice:** female
- **Screens:** none. **2D cartoon explainer animation** (reference: two DukaanDidi reels, "Ek coffee, chaar ghante"
  and "Rekha pico-fall").
- **Length:** about 95-100 s (follows the voice; 326 words)
- **Music:** none for now
- **Special wishes:** Same style as the reference reels.

## How the video is built

- 2D animation with the shared library in `animation/` (engine, sets, props, cast came from this reel). This
  folder keeps only the reel's own parts: `video/captions.js` (caption text + times), `video/scenes.js` (28 scenes),
  `video/cover.js`, `video/reel.json` (voice file).
- Check: `PY tools/render_reel.py reels/2026-11-13-hetal-lehenga-sunk-cost --sheet`; video: same without `--sheet`
  → `output/2026-11-13-hetal-lehenga-sunk-cost.mp4`; cover: `--cover`.
