# Reel: Ek link, poori dukaan

- **Date started:** 2026-11-23
- **Made by:** dhruvifewtone-wq
- **Status:** script approved

## Brief

- **About:** Meenaben sends 30 photos to every customer who writes "Photos bhejo na 🙏", and they still ask "Price
  kya hai?". She lists her shop on Anokhi Reet once (photos + rent price) and now sends one link: the customer sees
  everything, decides, and comes to the shop. Bonus: new customers find her shop in the app.
- **For:** Rental shop owners.
- **One message:** 30 photos nahi, bas ek link. Comment "SHOP".
- **Voice:** female (Vindemiatrix), as the narrator (teammate chose it, 2026-11-23).
- **Characters:** New: **Meenaben** (rental shop owner, busy and multitasking; a new character, not Meena from the
  cast, teammate chose it). Customers appear only as chat messages.
- **Length:** about 55 s (the voice decides).
- **Music:** none (voice only).
- **Special wishes:** WhatsApp-style chat screens as the main visual; yellow-box highlights on key words; emojis on
  screen as in the script.

  "no setup fee" or "5 minute" in our words (Never-say list). Voice: "Aur bonus? Naye customer bhi app pe unki dukaan
  dhoondh lete hain." Badges: WhatsApp se shuru • Apni city ke customers • Aasaan setup.
- Chat screens are WhatsApp-*style* (generic green and grey, no WhatsApp logo).
- The drawn shop page shows outfits with "Rent" labels but no amounts (no prices in our words).
- Voice only: the script's sounds (ping, camera clicks, sigh, ding, pops, chai sip, ching, jingle) are shown
