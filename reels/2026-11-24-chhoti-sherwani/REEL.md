- **Date started:** 2026-11-24
- **Made by:** dhruvifewtone-wq
- **Status:** voice approved

## Brief

- **About:** Riddhi's son Vihaan (5) wore a ₹6,000 sherwani once and it's already too small. Her drawer has four
  outfits like that (₹19,000 in all), each worn at one wedding. The same night, another mother, Krupa, panics: a
  wedding next week, and a new sherwani will be too small in two months. Riddhi has exactly that size, but they don't
  know each other. Riddhi photographs each outfit, writes the age and size, and lists them for rent; Krupa finds one
  on Anokhi Reet, her son looks like a little raja, and Riddhi's drawer starts earning.
- **For:** Mothers.
- **One message:** Your kids' outgrown outfits can go on rent for another mother. Comment "OUTFIT".
- **Voice:** female (Vindemiatrix), as the narrator; she also reads Krupa's line so there is no silent stretch.
- **Characters:** New: **Riddhi** (30s, mother), **Vihaan** (her 5-year-old son), **Krupa** (30s, another mother)
  and Krupa's son (about 5). The first kids in the cast.
- **Length:** about 55 s (the voice decides).
- **Music:** none (voice only).
- **Special wishes:** open loop (another mother with the same problem); the app name only at scene 8.
