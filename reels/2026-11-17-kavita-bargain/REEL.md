# Reel: Kavita aur Bargain (Episode 2)

- **Date started:** 2026-11-17
- **Made by:** teammate (script) + Claude
- **Status:** final (approved 2026-11-17)

## Brief

- **About:** A story reel. Kavita's rental shop is busy, but every customer bargains and she always gives in.
  At a mall she happily pays a fixed price because of "Buy 2 Get 1 Free", and learns the customer wants the
  feeling of winning, not a lower price (the "power of free", Shampanier, Mazar & Ariely 2007). She lists at fixed
  rent on Anokhi Reet, stops discounts and gives a free matching jewellery set instead.
- **For:** Rental shop, boutique and studio owners (vendors).
- **One message:** Customers don't want a discount, they want to feel they won. Fixed price + a small free gift.
- **Voice:** female
- **Characters:** new: **Kavita** (rental shop owner, 30s); customers (cast aunties `GUEST_W1`, `GUEST_W2`, a
  young customer); the experiment shown with props, not a drawing of the real researcher.
- **Length:** about 90-95 s (301 words)
- **Music:** none
- **Special wishes:** Script written by the teammate (Episode 2).
