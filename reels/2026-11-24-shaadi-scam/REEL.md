# Reel: Shaadi season ka sabse bada scam 🚨

- **Date started:** 2026-11-24
- **Made by:** dhruvifewtone-wq
- **Status:** script written (waiting for approval)

## Brief

- **About:** A playful crime-show parody. Inspector Bindiya calls buying an expensive outfit for one day "the biggest
  scam of the wedding season". She questions Hetal (₹40,000 lehenga, worn once), goes to her house for "evidence" and
  finds ₹83,000 of outfits in the almari, each worn once (Udaharan). Hetal realises the "scammer" was herself. The
  solution: rent the next outfit, and put the almari outfits on rent. Case closed; then the reveal: Anokhi Reet.
- **For:** Everyone: people who rent and people with full almaris.
- **One message:** Don't buy expensive for one day: rent it, and rent out what's in your almari. Comment "RENT".
- **Voice:** female (Vindemiatrix) as Inspector Bindiya, the narrator; she also reads Hetal's lines (speech bubbles
  show who speaks).
- **Characters:** New: **Inspector Bindiya** (detective coat, hat, torch). From the cast: **Hetal ben** (`HETAL`).
- **Length:** about 55 s (the voice decides).
- **Music:** none (voice only).
- **Special wishes:** crime-show look: red alert lights, case-file folders, "CONFIDENTIAL" stamps, a crime board with
  red strings, an interrogation lamp, evidence tags. Make it clear by scene 3 that the "scam" is a shopping habit,
  not a real fraud.
