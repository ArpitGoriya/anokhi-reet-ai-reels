# Reel: Sabne socha ₹50,000 ka hai…

- **Date started:** 2026-11-24
- **Made by:** dhruvifewtone-wq
- **Status:** script approved

## Brief

- **About:** At a wedding everyone guesses what Ishita's heavy maroon lehenga cost (₹40,000? ₹50,000? ₹60,000?). A
  week earlier she had only ₹8,000; she looked at her city's shops' outfits on her phone, set a budget and tried one
  on in a shop. The real price: ₹3,500, on rent; Mausi sits down in shock. The app (Anokhi Reet) is shown only at the
  end.
- **For:** Young women, wedding guests.
- **One message:** A look everyone remembers doesn't need a big budget: rent it. Comment your guess, and "PRICE".
- **Voice:** female (Vindemiatrix), as the narrator (as in all recent reels); she also reads the relatives' guesses so
  scene 3 isn't silent.
- **Characters:** New: **Ishita** (20s, the guest who rents) and **Mausi** (50s, her aunt). Cousins and Bhabhi from the
  cast (`KHUSHI`, `GUEST_W1`, `GUEST_W2`).
- **Length:** about 50 s (the voice decides).
- **Music:** none (voice only).
- **Special wishes:** open loop: the price tag stays "₹ ??" until scene 7; the app stays hidden (phone from the back)
  until scene 8.
