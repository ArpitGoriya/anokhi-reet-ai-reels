## Brief

- **About:** Priya buys a ₹19,000 lehenga for a cousin's wedding, wears it once, back in the almirah. Next wedding:
  "sabne photos mein dekh liya", buys again (₹38,000, worn 2 times). Open loop: "galti kahan hui?" Richard Thaler's
  mental accounting: money in separate jars; ₹19,000 feels normal in the "shaadi jar", but what did it cost per wear?
  The renter's fear ("fit nahi aaya toh? kaun hai owner?"), answered. The fix: rent the same designer lehenga on
  Anokhi Reet; 4 weddings, 4 looks, ₹10,000. Lesson + save/share/comment CTA.
- **For:** Women who buy a new lehenga for every wedding (renters). Goal: book wedding-season outfits.
- **One message:** You don't need to buy a lehenga; you need to look NEW at every function.
- **Voice:** female
- **Characters:** new: **Priya** (20s); wedding guests as small figures. Richard Thaler only as a name in a thought
  cloud (no drawing of a real person).
- **Length:** about 50 s (157 words)
- **Music:** none
- **Special wishes (PDF):** a counter fixed at the top-left the whole reel (Kharcha / Pehna) that changes
  ₹19,000·1 → ₹38,000·2 → green ₹10,000·Looks 4; hook text readable in 2 s; jars "Grocery / Bills / SHAADI";
  "₹19,000 ÷ 1 baar = ₹19,000 per look"; [Link in bio] [+ Follow] buttons at the end.
