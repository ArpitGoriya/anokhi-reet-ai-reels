## Brief

- **About:** An informative reel for the 2026 wedding season (21 Nov – 15 Dec): about 52 lakh weddings and about
  ₹90,000 crore of clothes, saree and garments business (CAIT estimate, Nov 2026). Everyone is busy, and the most
  demand is for outfits. Where is the rental shop's share? Customers search on their phones but can't find the shop.
  List it on Anokhi Reet through WhatsApp; the season ends 15 Dec (then Kamurta). Comment SHOP.
- **For:** Rental shop owners.
- **One message:** The season is huge and short: get your shop online before it ends. Comment "SHOP".

- **Characters:** From the cast: Kavya (beti) and Mummy (from "Cupboard full"), Papa (Kavya's papa: white kurta,
  glasses, a new outfit on the cast's Papa), Maheshbhai as the rental shop owner who isn't online yet. Extras:
  mehendi wali, caterer, photographer.
- **Length:** about 46 s (the voice decides).
- **Music:** none (voice only).
- **Special wishes (teammate):** informative; our characters, voice, tone and brand colours; keep people watching
  till the end.

## Lessons

- Captions longer than two lines fall into the bottom zone: split the chunk (`c10b`) and its WORDS.
- An `<img>` with `data-t` is centred on its left/top; give the centre point, not the corner.
- Keep decoration (rays, sparkles) off the logo, and use an official lockup for the reveal (for shop owners:
  `Anokhireet_shop_logo.svg` on maroon; `AnokhiReet_logo_maroon.svg` on cream).
