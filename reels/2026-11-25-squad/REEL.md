## Brief

- **About:** Bride Jhalak sends a dress code to her 7 bridesmaids (Haldi yellow, Mehendi green, Sangeet purple, Shaadi
  pink). Six are excited; Maya goes quiet: four new outfits aren't in her budget, so she writes "main nahi aa paungi".
  Jhalak calls: "Sab mujh pe chhod de." At the wedding Maya shines at every function; the friends ask how; Jhalak
  winks. Flashback: two weeks earlier Jhalak chose colour and budget for each function on Anokhi Reet and booked all
  four outfits on rent for Maya. "Sab rent pe hain!" Group hug. "Asli dosti mein koi peeche nahi chhootta."
- **For:** Young women, brides, bridesmaids, friend groups.
- **One message:** Real friends make sure nobody is left out; outfits on rent make it possible. Comment "RENT".
- **Voice:** female (Vindemiatrix) as the narrator; she also reads Jhalak's, Maya's and the friends' lines (speech
  bubbles and chat screens show who speaks).
- **Characters:** New: **Jhalak** (the bride), **Maya** (the quiet bridesmaid), and the six bridesmaids **Hetvi,
  Palak, Saloni, Vrunda, Meera, Disha** (shown in the chat, at the wedding and in the group hug).
- **Length:** about 60 s (the voice decides).
- **Music:** none (voice only).
- **Special wishes:** WhatsApp-style chat screens, wedding scenes, a sepia flashback. Maya's budget problem shown
  gently (quiet face, empty almari), never mocked. Hold Jhalak's wink for a full second with a sparkle.
