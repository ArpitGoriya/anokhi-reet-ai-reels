## Brief

- **About:** Jay turns up at every wedding in a new sherwani (Nov maroon, Dec ivory, Jan bandhgala, Feb jodhpuri). His
  friends think he's rich and guess ₹2-3 lakh. Twist 1: all four were rented, ₹1,500 each, ₹6,000 in all. Twist 2: his
  own wedding sherwani isn't locked in the almari; it goes out on rent and earns more than he spends (Udaharan:
  spent ₹6,000, earned ₹9,000). "Shaadi attend karke bhi profit mein hoon." Then Anokhi Reet: rent a sherwani, or
  put yours on rent. Comment OUTFIT.
