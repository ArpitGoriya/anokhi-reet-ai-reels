## Brief

- **About:** A story: Aarti gets a sangeet invitation two days before, with no outfit and no time. Her calm friend

- **Characters:** New: **Aarti** (young working woman), **Dimple** (her calm best friend, always has a plan),
  **Hetalben** (friendly rental shop owner in Ahmedabad; a new character, not the cast's Hetal ben from Surat).
  Friends at the sangeet: reuse `GUEST_W1`, `GUEST_W2`, `CUSTOMER_W`.

- **Special wishes:** Cold open at the sangeet, then rewind 48 hours. A small countdown timer in the top-left
  corner from scene 2 to scene 7 (48:00:00 → 24:00:00 → 00:00:00). "Kaise?" is answered only in scene 6.
  Yellow-box highlights on key words. Call to action: download Anokhi Reet.
