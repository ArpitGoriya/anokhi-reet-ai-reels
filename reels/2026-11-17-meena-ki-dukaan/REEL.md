## Brief

- **About:** A story reel. Meena's Ahmedabad rental shop has 200 lehengas, but only 18 went out this month: she
  sends photos on WhatsApp one by one, customers "think and tell", one lehenga got promised twice. Idea: unsold
  dates are lost forever, like an empty hotel room (perishable inventory). She lists everything on Anokhi Reet with
  photo, size and price, shares her online shop link, takes bookings online with dates, and customers come only to
  try. Now customers come from other areas too.
- **For:** Rental shop, boutique and studio owners.
- **One message:** The more your stock is seen, the more it earns. List it on Anokhi Reet.
- **Voice:** female
