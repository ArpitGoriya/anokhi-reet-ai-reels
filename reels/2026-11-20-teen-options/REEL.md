# Reel: 50 dikhaye… 0 bike. 3 dikhaye… booking! (Dukaan ka Funda)

- **Date started:** 2026-11-20
- **Made by:** dhruvifewtone-wq
- **Status:** script approved, voice take 1 made

## Brief

- **About:** Business lesson series "Dukaan ka Funda": too many choices stop people deciding. Shital shows a customer
  lehenga after lehenga and hears "Sochke batati hoon"; 20 customers, 4 bookings (Udaharan). The answer comes from a
  jam study (Iyengar & Lepper, 2000: 24 jams → 3% bought, 6 jams → 30%). She asks first, shows only 3, and suggests
  one: 12 bookings next week. Customers now come pre-filtered from Anokhi Reet.
- **For:** Rental shop owners.
- **One message:** Zyada nahi, sahi options bikte hain. Comment "SHOP".
- **Voice:** female (Vindemiatrix), as the narrator (same as the series).
- **Characters:** New: **Shital** (young rental shop owner) and **Khushi** (customer). Study crowd from the cast.
- **Length:** about 60-70 s (the voice decides).
- **Music:** none (voice only).
- **Special wishes:** Yellow-box highlights, emojis on screen, open loops (jam, the guess, the held-back number),
  "STEP 1/2/3" with step 3 called the most important.
