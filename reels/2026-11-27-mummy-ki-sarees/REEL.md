# Reel: Beta, inki bhi photo achhi kheechna… 📸🥻

- **Date started:** 2026-11-27
- **Made by:** dhruvifewtone-wq
- **Status:** script written (waiting for approval)

## Brief

- **About:** Mummy wants to sell the daughter's wedding lehenga (₹40,000, worn once) for just ₹3,000 to make space in
  the almari. The daughter says "ek try karne do": photos, size, rent, listed online for rent. Month 1: two bookings,
  month 2: three. Twist: three months later Mummy sneaks into her room with her own 15 sarees: "Beta, inki bhi photo
  achhi kheechna… aur rent thoda zyada rakhna!" Now nobody at home sells; everyone rents out. Anokhi Reet: list your
  outfits for rent, comment RENT.
- **For:** Young women 20-32 (and their mummies) with wedding outfits in the almari.
- **One message:** Don't sell your wedding outfits cheap: list them for rent on Anokhi Reet. Comment "RENT".
- **Voice:** female (Vindemiatrix) as the daughter (narrator); she also reads Mummy's lines (speech bubbles show who
  speaks). Female Hindi grammar.
- **Characters:** New: **Khushi, the beti** (pink suit), **Mummy** (orange saree, hair in a bun).
- **Length:** about 60 s (the voice decides).
- **Music:** none (voice only).
- **Special wishes:** cream background; a pink end card.
