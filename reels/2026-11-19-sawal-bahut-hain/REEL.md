## Brief

- **About:** Script 3 for rental shop owners. A doubtful shop owner's questions pop up as bubbles over his head;
  an Anokhi Reet team member answers each one and the bubble pops, until the screen is clear and he lists his shop.
- **For:** Rental shop owners (lehengas, sherwanis) who are curious but unsure. Meant as a pinned post.
- **One message:** Whatever your doubt, listing your shop is simple: ask us in the comments or write "LIST".
- **Voice:** female (Vindemiatrix), as the narrator (switched from male after hearing male take 1, 2026-11-19).
- **Characters:** New: **Hareshbhai** (50s, careful, slightly doubtful rental shop owner) and **Neha** (20s,
  friendly Anokhi Reet team member). Renters on phones in scene 5 (reuse `CUSTOMER_W`, `GUEST_W1`, `GUEST_M`).
- **Length:** about 50-55 s (the voice decides).
- **Music:** none (voice only).
- **Special wishes:** Flat 2D, brand colours, yellow-box highlights on key words. Question bubbles pop up over
  Hareshbhai's head, crowd the screen, and each one pops when answered. Emojis on screen as in the script.
  Call to action: comment a question or write "LIST" + anokhireet.in.

## Lessons

- The first line has to name the viewer and promise a payoff ("har rental shop owner ke 5 sawal… 5th sabse
  important"); a generic "Sawal bahut hain?" is weaker.
- Without the fee lines, a cost answer still needs an action: "WhatsApp pe seedha pucho" + a chat on screen.
- Short Hindi negatives can get swallowed by the voice ("पसंद न आया" sounded like "पसंद आया"): use "नहीं", and check
  key words with the speech recogniser before handing over.
- Whisper often loses track of long middle stretches of this voice: check STARTS against the pauses
  (ffmpeg silencedetect) and set those captions by hand.
- Keep the shop sign out of shots with a big two-line title at the top.
