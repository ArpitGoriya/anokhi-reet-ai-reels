## Brief

- **About:** A young man wears the same maroon sherwani to three weddings; his friends name him "Repeat wala ladka"
  (shaadi 1 "mast sherwani!", shaadi 2 "ye wahi hai na?", shaadi 3 he's cropped out of the group photo). Before the
  fourth wedding he rents a royal blue sherwani online: his friends go quiet, the photographer poses only him, and his
  crush asks where the look is from. "Rent par! Tu bhi lehenga, saree, chaniya choli, sab rent par le sakti hai."
  "Toh agli shaadi matching karenge!" Now he's "Look wala ladka". Anokhi Reet: rent the outfit, comment LOOK.
- **For:** Young men and women, 18-30, who repeat their wedding outfits.
- **One message:** A new look at every wedding: rent it on Anokhi Reet. Comment "LOOK".
- **Voice:** **both approved voices** (teammate chose it, 2026-11-27): the ladka (narrator, and his friends' quoted
  lines) in the male voice (Sadachbia); the crush's two lines in the female voice (Vindemiatrix); each unchanged,
  joined into one track. Who says what: [`voice-parts.txt`](voice-parts.txt) (L = ladka, C = crush).
- **Characters:** New: **the ladka** (maroon sherwani → royal blue sherwani), **3 dost** (orange, green, purple
  kurtas), **the crush** (pink suit), **a photographer**.
- **Length:** about 60-66 s (the voice decides).
- **Music:** none (voice only).
- **Special wishes:** peach background, a marigold toran and a red-gold wedding arch; captions word by word, key words
  in yellow.
