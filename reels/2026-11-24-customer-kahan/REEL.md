## Brief

- **About:** A short, direct talk to rental shop owners: the shop is full of outfits, but the day goes by waiting for
  customers (pamphlets and status tried). Today's customer searches on the phone, and that customer is on Anokhi Reet.
  "Customer humare paas hai; aap bas dukaan list karo." Listing through WhatsApp, customers from your city, easy setup.
- **For:** Rental shop owners.
- **One message:** We bring the customers; you just list your shop. Comment "SHOP".
- **Voice:** female (Vindemiatrix), as a presenter from the Anokhi Reet team talking to the camera.
- **Characters:** New: a **team member** from Anokhi Reet (the presenter, maroon kurti, phone in hand). From the cast:
  Maheshbhai (`MAHESH`, a shop owner who isn't online), Khushi (`KHUSHI`, a customer searching on her phone).
- **Length:** about 28 s (short reel; the voice decides).
- **Music:** none (voice only).
- **Special wishes:** quick cuts, one line per shot, word-by-word captions with key words in gold; an app "screen
  recording" feel on line 4 (shops and outfits scrolling on the phone).

## Lessons

- A maroon-dressed presenter disappears on a maroon backdrop: keep the studio backdrop light (beige).
- A talking-to-camera presenter needs to be big (`bust` scale about 1.45) with a desk in front, not small at the bottom.
- Long captions (more than about 30 letters) wrap to three lines and fall into the bottom zone: split them (`c9b`).
