## Brief

- **About:** A story: a rental shop owner dreams of a busy season, wakes up to an empty shop, finds Anokhi Reet
  on his phone, lists his shop, and the dream starts coming true.
- **For:** Rental shop owners (ethnic wear: lehengas, sherwanis).
- **One message:** List your rental shop on Anokhi Reet so customers find your outfits on their phones.
- **Voice:** female (Vindemiatrix), as the narrator.
- **Characters:** New: **Pankajbhai**, rental shop owner, hardworking and hopeful. Customers with phones
  (reuse `BRIDE`, `GUEST_W1`, `GUEST_W2`, `GUEST_M` in new outfits where it fits).
- **Length:** about 55 s (the voice decides).
- **Music:** none (voice only).
- **Special wishes:** Dream vs reality: dream scenes have a soft dreamy glow, sparkles and wavy edges; real
  scenes are plain. Scene 7 replays the dream's moments without the glow. Yellow-box highlights on key words.
  Call to action: comment "LIST".
