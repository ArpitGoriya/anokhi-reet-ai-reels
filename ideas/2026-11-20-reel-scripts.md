# Reel scripts: 12 ideas (2026-11-20)

Written by Claude for dhruvifewtone-wq. Each one is a full script: pick one and say "make this reel"; it then goes
through the normal steps (brand check, Devanagari voice script, voice, video).

How they keep people watching: every hook opens a question (a mystery, a count, "what happened next?") that is only
answered near the end; every script shows a common problem, then a simple solution, then a payoff.

Brand check done for all: no Anokhi Reet prices, discounts, number claims or guarantees; story numbers are marked
"Udaharan"; app features only as shown in earlier reels (browse your city's shops by city, occasion and budget; see
photos and rent; compare; list outfits for rent); voice only, no "whoosh". Narrator: female voice (third person).
Characters from the cast where possible.

## Suggested order

1. **#1 (4 type ke guest):** widest reach (people tag friends).
2. **#2 (Mausi):** comedy, shareable, cast already exists.
3. **#10 (daag lag gaya):** "save" bait plus a real fear people search for.
4. **#4 (Nisha Part 3):** keeps the series going.
5. **#11 (late return):** strong for shop owners.
